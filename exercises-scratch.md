Chapter 25
Exercise 1: Translate the following
Questions:
1. Mpinga mu gitondo.  Ariko bariya batazi guhinga sinshaka ko baza.
2. Nza mw ishuri iteka.  Nkunda kwiga ibintu byinshi abantu benshi batazi.
3. Shyira imboga mw isahane nini.  Inyama itarimo, ukwiriye kuyishyira mu muriro.
4. Ntuhora ubikora utyo ni cyo gituma batagushima.
5. Bafite imfunguzo z'umwigisha.
6. Uyu muntu udafite icyo kurya aragushaka.
7. Ntitumukunda kuko atavuga neza.
8. Ntukora neza kuko udafite imbaraga.
9. Abantu bafite imfunguzo bazashobora kwinjira mu rusengero.  Abatazifite ntibazinjiramo.
10. Mpora mvuga mu rurimi rwanjye gusa.  Sinzi urwawe.
11. Azavuga yuko atakuzi.  Nanjye nzavuga yuko ntamuzi.
12. Mfite urwandiko rwe rutavuga neza.  Sinshaka ko urusoma.
13. Impinja zikunda gusinzira cyane.  Abantu bakuru ntibasinzira igihe cyose.
14. Urufunguzo rwabo bajya barubika munsi ya ruriya rubaho.  Imfunguzo zanjye ndazifite.
15. Sinshaka guhora nkubwira yuko udakwiriye kujya mu rugo rwabo.

Answers:
1. I hoe in the morning.  But those (people) who do not know how to hoe I don't want to come (lit. that they come)
2. I always come to school.  I enjoy learning about many things many people do not know (about)
3. Put the vegetables in the large bowl.  The meat which is not in it, you should put in the fire.
4. You don't always do it like that; that's why they don't thank/praise you.
5. They have the teacher's keys.
6. This person who does not have any food is wanting you.
7. We don't like him, because he does not speak well.
8. You don't work well because you don't have strength.
9. The people who have the keys will be able to enter into the church.  Those who do not have them will not enter.
10. I always speak only in my language.  I don't know yours.
11. She will say that she does not know you.  And I shall say that I don't know her.
12. I have his/her letter which does not read (speak) well.  I don't want you to read it.
13. Infants like to sleep a lot.  Grown-ups don't sleep all the time.
14. They always keep their key under that board.  I have my keys.
15. I don't want to be having to tell you constantly that you must not go to their home.



Exercise 2: Extra Sentences: Kinyarwanda to Engliah
Questions:
1. None ndaruhuka, ariko ejo nzabafasha kuko nshaka kurangiza imirimo yose muri iki cyumweru
2. Akunda kwibuka imigani ya kera no kuyandika kugira ngo twese tuyimenye.
3. Ukwiriye kubabwira yuko ntazaza ejo.
4. Nzi ko ntazabirangiza ni cyo gitumye ndashaka kubitangira.
5. Sindamushima.  N'arangiza ni bwo nzamushima.
6. Ibisambo ntibiragerageza kwiba ibintu bye kuko bitazi yuko atari imuhira.
7. Abantu bose ntibamushaka kuko atazi gukora.
8. Abantu benshi basinzira nijoro, ariko bamwe ni bwo bakora.
9. Ajya arangiza imirimo ye yose atarataha kuko ari umuntu witonda.
10. Ntibashaka guhora batubwira yuko bazabirangiza ejo.
11. Bika ibintu byose kuri gahunda nziza kugira ngo abandi babibone ni babishaka.
12. Sindamenya izina rye.  Ntibarambwira amazina y'abazaza mw iteraniro.
13. Sinshobora gukora none kuko ntarasinzira.

Answers:
1. I'm resting today, but tomorrow I shall help you/them because I want to finish all the jobs/work this week.
2. He loves to remember the stories of old and write them down so that we all might know them.
3. You must tell them that I will not come tomorrow.
4. I know that I will not finish it; that's why I don't want to begin it.
5. I haven't thanked him yet.  When he finishes, that's when I'll thank him.
6. The thieves have not tried to steal his things because they don't know that he's not home.
7. Everyone doesn't want him because he doesn't know how to work.
8. Many people sleep at night, but that's when some work.
9. She always finishes all her work before she goes home because she is a careful person.
10. They don't want to always be telling us that they will finish it tomorrow.
11. Put away the things properly so that others can find them when they want them.
12. I don't know his name.  They haven't told me yet the names of those who will attend the meeting.
13. I'm unable to work today because I haven't slept yet.

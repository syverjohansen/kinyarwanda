Exercise 2: Translate to English
Questions:
1. Ntabwo bazi n'itangiriro ryo gusoma
2. Dukwiriye kugura udutabo cumi na kamwe two gusoma
3. Nshima data yuko mfite umutima wo gufasha abandi.
4. Mfite ibyifuzo byo kubikoresha ngo mbone umumaro wabyo
5. Azakwereka inzira yo kuzamuka umusozi.
6. Mfite inama yo kugenda vuba, kuko urugendo ruzaba rurerure
7. Umwigisha aratwigisha iby'akamaro ko kutanywa itabi
8. Arazana mw ishuri udutabo mirongwitatu na tubiri two gusoma.
9. Har'inzira esheshatu zo kujya imuhira
10. Umufundi aratwereka uburyo bushya bwo gukoresha icyuma
11. Iyi mbwa ntizi uburyo bwo kunkurikira
12. Bafite agahinda ko kugenda
13. Uyu muti umpa imbaraga zo gukomeza.

Answers:
1. They don't even know the elements of reading
2. We should buy 11 little books to read.
3. I thank my father that I have a heart to help others
4. I have the desire to use them so as to discover their value (use)
5. He will show you the path (way) to climb the mountain
6. I plan (have a plan) to go soon, because they journey will be long
7. The teacher is teaching us about the value of not smoking tobacco.
8. She will bring to school 32 little books to read.
9. There are six ways to go home
10. The craftsman is showing us a new way to use iron.
11. This dog doesn't know how to follow me.
12. They are sorry (have sorrow) to leave (to go)
13. This medicine gives me the strength to continue.
